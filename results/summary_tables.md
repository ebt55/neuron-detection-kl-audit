## 8. Limitations
- **Model scale & architecture boundary:** This audit evaluates dense `Qwen/Qwen3-1.7B` (SwiGLU MLP neurons) rather than Goodfire's 35B MoE; dense 1.7B neurons are substantially more polysemantic than large MoE experts.
- **Nearest same-layer neuron (descriptive, Tier 0 only) cosine strength:** Dense 1.7B same-layer nearest `w_gate` neighbours have modest cosine similarity (`median=0.174` across 50 sampled neurons; `median=0.155` across `308` surviving Tier-0 pairs / `40` poles / `29` neurons), so this arm is strictly descriptive rather than a high-cosine near-duplicate stress test.
- **Goodfire `<p90` bipolar opposite-pole firing:** Filtering only the target pole `< p90` leaves the opposite pole firing `>= p90` on `66/600` (`11.0%`) and `>= p97` on `18/600` (`3.0%`) of distractor target tokens.
- **Clean-window selection effect in weak bands:** Filtering out non-target context tokens `>= p90` isolates the marked weak-band token cleanly, but selects the subset of weak-band occurrences without strong co-occurring context peaks. Exact corpus survival shares are reported directly below (and the unfiltered exploratory ablation in Section 9 quantifies the context-peak effect directly).
